! Before you meet, stay safe

  • Verify someone with a short video call before sharing personal details or making plans to meet.
  • Keep early conversations on Love Wonders rather than moving off-platform right away.
  • Never send money or gift cards to someone you haven't met in person — this is a common scam pattern.
