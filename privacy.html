Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and the controls you have over it — without the legalese fog.

← Back home About Contact Terms Privacy

1 What we collect

  • Account info — your email (or Google account) via Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile details — whatever you choose to fill in: name, bio, photo, age, gender, location, languages, relationship intent, interests, and similar fields.
  • Messages — the conversations you have with other members, so threads can be delivered and displayed.
  • Activity — a "last active" timestamp that powers the Online-now indicator, shown to others only if you've enabled it.
  • Reports — anything you report, or that's reported about you, so our team can review it.
  • Notifications — a device token, only if you opt into browser push notifications.

2 What we don't collect

  • No GPS or precise location tracking — city/country are only what you type in yourself.
  • No third-party ad trackers, and we don't sell your data to anyone.
  • We don't scan your messages to build an advertising profile of you.

3 How it's used

Your details power Discover's matching — weighing interests, intent, languages and your filters to surface people worth meeting. Messages and notifications exist to run the chat itself. Reports are used to keep the community safe. Your theme and accent-color choices are saved only in your own browser's local storage, never on our servers.

4 Your private ID code

Certain optional details — extra photos, social links — can be locked behind a private ID code that only you set. That content is encrypted on your own device (AES-GCM) before it's ever sent to us, so it's unreadable on our servers without your code. Renewing your code re-encrypts that content, which is also why an old code stops working once you do.

5 Who can see what

  • Your public profile fields are visible to other 18+ members according to Discover's filters.
  • Members you block can no longer see your profile or message you.
  • Online-now status is visible to others only if you've turned that setting on.
  • Messages in a thread are visible only to the two people in it. They're transmitted over an encrypted connection and stored in our database — this isn't end-to-end encryption the way the ID-code-locked content above is, so please avoid sending anything you wouldn't want stored.

6 Where it's stored

Love Wonders runs on Firebase (Google Cloud) infrastructure. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest using Firebase's own storage encryption.

7 Your controls

  • Edit or clear most profile fields any time from your Profile page.
  • Toggle whether your online status is visible to others.
  • Block or report a member directly from their profile.
  • Renew your private ID code whenever you'd like to re-lock your protected content.
  • Email us to request full deletion of your account and message history.

8 Notifications

If you enable browser push notifications, we use them to alert you to new messages and matches. You can revoke this permission at any time through your browser's site settings.

9 An 18+ platform

Love Wonders is not intended for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect data from minors. If we learn an account belongs to someone underage, it will be removed.

10 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date below. Continuing to use Love Wonders afterward means you accept the update.

11 Contact
