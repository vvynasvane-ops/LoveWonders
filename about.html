About Love Wonders

A quiet corner of the internet for people who'd rather feel understood before they're seen.

← Back home About Contact Terms Privacy

✦ Why we built this

Most apps are built for the swipe — optimized for speed, not for whether two people actually fit. Love Wonders was built the other way around. Your profile stays dim until you choose to be known, matches are surfaced on what you're actually looking for, and there's no leaderboard telling you how many people liked you back. It's less arcade, more observatory: a slower, quieter way of finding someone worth the wait.

✦ How it works

Discover looks past distance alone — it weighs relationship intent, interests, languages and the filters you set to quietly surface people worth meeting, instead of handing you an endless deck. Conversations only open once there's mutual interest, and whether you show up as "Online now" to others is entirely your call — off by default until you decide otherwise.

🔒

Privacy by default

Extra details you choose to share can be locked behind your own private ID code, encrypted before they ever leave your device.

🛰

Verified & vigilant

Block and Report are one tap away on every profile, and a real person reads every report that comes in.

🌙

Slow by design

No streaks, no urgency meters. Wonder is meant to build gradually, not be farmed for engagement.

🪐

An adults-only orbit

Every member confirms they're 18 or older before they can join — no exceptions.

✦ Who's behind it

Love Wonders is built and looked after by a small, independent team — the kind that reads its own support inbox. If something feels off, breaks, or could simply be kinder, we want to hear about it.
